*Last updated: 20 October 2026*

This policy explains what personal information StarBlaze Ventures ("we", "us") collects through this website, why, and what choices you have.

## 1. What We Collect

- **Enquiries.** If you use the contact form, the form opens WhatsApp with your name, your chosen topic, and your message ready to send. We receive that information only when you send the message. If you call, email, or message us on WhatsApp, we receive whatever details you share, including your phone number.
- **Purchases.** If you buy through our Store, payment is handled by our payment gateway. We receive your order details and contact information, but not your full card details.
- **Technical data.** Like any website, our hosting provider may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers on this site at the time of writing.

## 2. Why We Collect It

To respond to your enquiries, deliver the products and services you buy, provide support, meet legal and accounting obligations, and keep the website working and secure.

## 3. Who We Share It With

- **Third-party payment providers** to process purchases.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)** when you contact us through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
- **Hosting and content providers.** This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may receive your IP address and technical data when you visit, under their own privacy policies.
- **Authorities**, where we are legally required to.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 4. How Long We Keep It

We keep enquiry and order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, and then delete or anonymise them.

## 5. Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw consent you have given, by contacting us at {{ business.contact.email }} or {{ business.contact.phoneDisplay }}. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable Indian law.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
